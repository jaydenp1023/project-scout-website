Privacy Policy

Clear information about how this early website works.

Last updated: November 6, 2026 · Public preview website

Information you share

If you create an account, Supabase processes your email and password for authentication. We may store your optional business name, trade, city, and state to remember your preferences. If you email or call us, we use the information you share to respond. Payment testing takes place on Stripe-hosted Sandbox checkout. We do not handle or store card numbers on our own servers, and no real payments are accepted during this test.

Website and account providers

GitHub Pages hosts this website, and Supabase provides account authentication and profile storage. These providers process information needed to deliver their services. A browser session may be stored on your device to keep you signed in. We have not intentionally added advertising trackers to this page.

How we use your details

We use account information to sign you in and save your preferences, and correspondence to respond to requests. We do not sell your correspondence to advertisers. Please avoid sending sensitive financial or identity information by ordinary email.

Your questions and requests

If you want to ask about correspondence you've sent or request that we delete it, email projectscout.business@gmail.com. We will review your request and respond.

Future services

We will update this policy before enabling real paid subscriptions, automated alerts, or other account services. These policies are preliminary and should be reviewed before public launch.

Terms of Use

Please read these notes about the Project Scout public preview.

Last updated: November 6, 2026 · Public preview website

What Project Scout is

Project Scout is an independent service designed to help people discover publicly advertised government bidding opportunities. We are not a government agency and do not issue, manage, or award the solicitations we link to.

Verify opportunities yourself

Preview listings may be limited, delayed, or outdated. Always verify the bid deadline, eligibility rules, documents, addenda, and current status with the issuing agency before deciding to bid. Project Scout does not guarantee eligibility, contract awards, or business results.

Subscription availability

Prices and features shown are prelaunch proposals. Stripe Sandbox checkout is for testing only; no real payments are accepted. Public paid subscriptions are not yet available. Subscription terms, cancellation details, and any applicable refund information will be provided before paid service launches.

Acceptable use

Please use the public preview lawfully and don't attempt to disrupt the site or impersonate others when contacting us.
