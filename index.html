Common questions

Quick answers, no runaround.

Does Project Scout award contracts?

No. Government agencies publish and award their own contracts. Project Scout is an independent discovery tool that helps you locate public solicitations and follow the official source links.

Are the government bids exclusive?

No. These are publicly posted opportunities. The value of Project Scout is making them easier to discover, organize, and revisit.

What area does Project Scout cover?

Our initial focus is Texas and its supported public procurement sources. Coverage varies by agency, and we do not claim every city or county bid is included.

Can I bid on every contract listed?

Not necessarily. Each agency sets qualifications, registration requirements, insurance, licenses, and deadlines. Check the official bid notice to confirm your eligibility.

Can I subscribe today?

Not yet. We're finishing secure customer access and verifying the experience before opening subscriptions. This preview does not collect payment or account information.
